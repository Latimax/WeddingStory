To My Bride, Grace Mercy ❤️

From Gideon

My dearest Grace Mercy, my Damsel, my sunshine,

As I look forward to becoming your husband, one thing is clear in my heart: I don't just want to marry you, I want to build a lifetime with you.

I want to be the man you can trust, the friend you can confide in, the partner who stands beside you, and the husband who keeps choosing you, even when life gets difficult. I know love is more than beautiful words, it is patience, sacrifice, faithfulness, understanding, and the daily decision to show up for each other.

My desire is for us to build a home where Christ is honoured, love is evident, peace is nurtured, and both of us can grow into all God has called us to be. I want us to pray together, dream together, face challenges together, and never forget that we are on the same team.

I don't promise to be a perfect husband, but I promise to keep growing, to take responsibility when I'm wrong, to listen when you need to be heard, and to love you with intention. I want my actions to give meaning to my words and my presence to remind you that you are not alone.

Grace, I am not just looking forward to our wedding day, I am looking forward to every day after it. The ordinary mornings, the difficult seasons, the answered prayers, the laughter, the lessons, and the beautiful life we will build by God's grace.

You are the woman I want beside me as we navigate life, build our home, and fulfil God's purpose for us. My prayer is that, many years from now, we will look back with grateful hearts and know that we chose love, honoured our covenant, and kept God at the centre of it all.

I choose you, Grace Mercy not only for the beautiful moments, but for the journey, the growth, and the lifetime ahead of us. And by God's grace, I will keep choosing you.

With all my love,

Your husband-to-be,
Gideon Oluwatobiloba Ofuegbe ❤️

Open Letter From Grace-Mercy to Gideon

From Grace-Mercy

Oluwatobiloba mi, my pure love,

Sometimes, I sit back and think about us, and I can not help but smile at how beautifully our story has unfolded.

I remember that night I officially said YES to you! Oh, yes, I remember it so well. I remember the night we spoke on the phone for the very first time, and you told me you had been sent to kidnap me. 😂❤️ Who would have thought that a conversation that began with such an unusual statement would become the beginning of something this beautiful?

And the funny thing is, I flowed so well with you that night.. and I blushed a little.. 🤭

I remember the first time you told me you loved me, especially because, at the time, you could almost have been brother-zoned. Righttttt.. 😂

I remember our long nights of conversations, prayers, and studying the Word together.

I remember how you taught me the difference between a calling and an assignment. There are many things you have said to me that I may not be able to recall word for word, but there is one explanation that has stayed with me. You said, "Your calling is that purpose, and it entails many assignments, specific, direct instructions from God."

And then, there was that day I was so angry with you. I remember how upset I was and how I probably overreacted. Yet, in the middle of it all, you simply said, "I am sorry." Looking back, I realise that beyond the disagreement itself, there was something beautiful in that moment. It reminded me that love is not always about proving who is right. Sometimes, it is about choosing peace, extending grace, and protecting what matters more than winning an argument. I got that point!

My pure love, I know that marriage will not automatically make everything perfect. We will have days when we understand each other effortlessly and days when we will need to try a little harder.

But one thing I am certain of is this: with God by our side, our PURELOVE union will become a beautiful testimony of His faithfulness and a blessing to the world.

Gideon, I promise to love you, respect you, and be genuinely proud of you. I promise to choose you, to keep admiring the man you are, and to never become so familiar with your presence that I forget what a gift it is to have you in my life.

I pray that the Lord will crown your diligence with honour and great success. Gideon, please get ready to have a PURE marriage and ENJOY marriage with me! 😂🤍

My Gideon, Ifeteminikansoso, I love you deeply.

Your Heaven's gift,
Grace-Mercy.

❤️ Covenant Reflection

What Marriage Means to Us

In Gideon's unedited words to his bride:

“My wife, when you ask me what kind of marriage I want with you, my answer is simple: I want a marriage where God is at the centre and we are genuinely on the same team.

I want us to always be best friends, partners, lovers, prayer partners, and companions for life. I want you to feel safe with me, free to talk, cry, disagree, and tell me when I've hurt you without fearing that I will stop loving you.

I want us to communicate, apologise when we are wrong, forgive freely, and learn from our mistakes. I want us to fight for each other, not against each other. When there is a problem, it should be us against the problem, not you against me.

I want to lead our home through love, sacrifice, responsibility, and service. I want to value your wisdom, support your dreams, and see you become everything God has called you to be.

I want us to pray, study God's Word, laugh, travel, build, and serve God together. I want us to keep flirting when we're old and still make each other blush years after our wedding. 😂❤️

When children come, I want us to raise them together and show them what love, faithfulness, respect, and forgiveness look like.

I know we won't always get everything right. There will be difficult seasons, disagreements, and days when we are not at our best. But I want us to keep choosing each other because our marriage is worth protecting.

Above everything, I want our home to welcome God's presence, reflect Christ, and fulfil His purpose. I want us to build a life together that honours Him.

I don't just want to marry you, Grace-Mercy. I want to do life with you, grow old with you, and look back on every season with gratitude that I chose you.

It's not a perfect marriage, but a God-built marriage.
Our marriage. ❤️”

Two lives. One covenant. Christ at the centre. A lifetime of choosing each other.
