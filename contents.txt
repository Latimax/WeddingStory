🤍 Our Love Story

Two lives. One covenant. A love built with God.

2023 | The Beautiful Damsel 💕

Around 2023, while serving the Lord in church, I sighted a pretty damsel who walked past me. She was beautiful and thriving in the Lord. We weren't in talking terms then, so I simply admired her from a distance. Little did I know that the beautiful damsel I kept eyeing would one day become my wife. 😂❤️

2024 | Your Beauty Is Killing! 📸

Fast forward to 2024, we began exchanging casual greetings and having short conversations whenever we saw each other in church.

I remember one day when she was outside taking pictures with her friends. I asked for her permission to take a picture of the beautiful damsel I was seeing. As a sharp guy, I was only trying to get a little closer. 😂 Thankfully, she was courteous and kind enough to say, "No problem," and I took a picture of her with my phone.

Afterwards, I sent the picture to her on WhatsApp with the caption, "Your beauty is killing." 😂❤️

Her response? A sticker that read, "Laughing in the Holy Ghost!" 😂😂

As expected from a sister in the Lord!

Interestingly, there were no feelings attached at the time. We simply continued with our occasional greetings and conversations, completely unaware of what God was preparing.

January 2025 | The Kidnapper's First Call 📞❤️

Fast forward to January 2025, we started talking properly, and I remember our first phone call that night. I playfully told her that I had been sent to kidnap her. 😂

She was super engaging, and the conversation flowed so naturally that we kept talking until we eventually said goodnight.

That was the beginning of something beautiful. Our conversations grew, our friendship deepened, and we began to share more of ourselves with each other through long talks, prayers, and studying the Word of God together.

Somewhere along the way, friendship began to take on a deeper meaning. ❤️

The Divine Confirmation | When God Stepped In 🙏🏽

At some point, Gideon shared his convictions with me about two occasions when he believed God had told him that I was his wife.

I knew I couldn't afford to take such a decision lightly, so I took a break from our conversations to seek the face of God for two weeks.

During that period, I began to understand that God had brought him into my life as my husband, even though I fought the idea a little. 😂❤️

But as I continued praying, the Lord kept confirming His Word and the convictions I had received.

Eventually, I said yes to him, and we began our journey together.

The YES | A Beautiful Beginning 💍

And so, our journey began! Not necessarily an easy one, but one that has taught us, stretched us, and given us opportunities to grow in love, patience, understanding, and faith.

We have had beautiful moments, learned important lessons, shared laughter, prayed together, and continued to discover what it means to love intentionally.

Through it all, one thing has remained important to us: keeping God at the centre of our love story.

PURELOVE’26 | From a Love Story to a Covenant 🤍💍

And here we are, looking forward to December 5, 2026, when we will stand before God and our loved ones to begin a new chapter as husband and wife.

What started with a beautiful damsel, a picture, and the words "Your beauty is killing" has grown into a story of friendship, divine direction, love, and the beautiful journey of becoming one.

As we look forward to our wedding, our hearts are filled with gratitude for how far God has brought us and excitement for all that lies ahead.

Our prayer is that our love will be more than a beautiful story. May our marriage become a testimony of God's faithfulness, a home where His presence dwells, and a union through which His purpose is fulfilled.

This is our story. This is our PURELOVE. And by God's grace, this is only the beginning. 🤍✨
